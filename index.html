Agent Activity Timeline

See what your agents are doing. Know what you can trust.

A plain-language timeline of everything an agent does on a task, with every result marked as verified by Ziki or reported by the agent.

How it works See the features

Everything an agent does, in one place

Built for people who aren’t developers, so you can follow the work and stay in control without reading a log.

Follow along in plain language

See what an agent is doing right now, what it found and what it changed, without reading a single log.

Verified or reported, never blurred

Every result says who confirmed it. Verified by Ziki means Ziki checked it. Reported means only the agent says so.

Decide with the full picture

When an agent needs you, it shows the decision, the reason, the urgency and what will change. Approve, reject or ask back.

Finish with proof

Done comes with what was reported, what was verified, what is still open and the evidence behind it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open the timeline

    Every task has one, in the order things happened, with the current step highlighted.

  2. 2

    Look closer

    Expand any step for findings, evidence links and check results. Technical detail stays one click away.

  3. 3

    Respond when asked

    Approve, reject or add a note. Your answer and the agent’s next steps land in the same timeline.

  4. 4

    Check the outcome

    Ziki’s verification sits beside the agent’s report, so you can see what is confirmed.
