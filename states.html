Trust labels

A second axis, separate from status. It answers one question: who has actually confirmed this?

Verified by Ziki

Ziki checked the result itself, on its own, and says what it looked at.

Reported by Nia

The agent says it happened. Ziki hasn’t confirmed it. Shown with a dashed outline, never a tick.

Ziki is checking

Reported by the agent, with a check under way. Turns into one of the other labels.

Didn’t hold up

The agent reported it, but Ziki found something different. For example: “Nia said an email was sent. Ziki found none.”
